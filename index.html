Campus Control

Our integrated automation suite — schedule-driven door control, event & facility approvals, and emergency lockdown — all reading from one master schedule that auto-adjusts for weather, calendar events, and athletics.

Replaces: manual lock/unlock rounds, emailed facility requests, and PA-only lockdowns.

Campus Presence

Sign-in / sign-out on two iPad kiosks at the visitor entrances, and the live answer to who is on campus right now. Visitors badge themselves in; students sign in late or out early; a hard authorised-pickup check turns a stranger’s attempt into an office flag rather than a sign-out. Presence is derived from an append-only event log, so the board, the printable muster and the lockdown screens all read one source.

Replaces: a paper clipboard at each entrance, a phone call to the classroom, and somebody’s memory of who is allowed to collect which child.

Directory Search

Type a name, a parent's email, a subject or a grade and get the answer as you type — student, guardian and teacher records, class rosters, emergency contacts, and each student's schedule with the class they're in right now highlighted, plus an absent/late badge straight from attendance. Room schedules show what's in every classroom today and which ones are free right now.

Replaces: logging into the SIS (or asking the office) for a phone number, an emergency contact, or where a student is this period.

Student Portal

A phone-first app each student signs into with their school account. One list of what is due, built from homework the teacher typed in prose, lesson plans and the gradebook, with every item given a date (“due Thursday”, “quiz 9/29”, or the next school day). The week’s schedule with the class meeting right now, bell delays and closed days honoured; scores and class averages; attendance; and for athletes, today’s game with the dismissal time read out of the coach’s calendar note.

Replaces: a student checking six class pages in the SIS, a homework note that vanished by Tuesday, and asking a coach what time the bus leaves.

Dismissal Board

Who is in each bus, van and car lane at 3:10 — and who not to wait for. Four lanes (Bus · Car · Early Bird · Staff Kid) built nightly from the SIS, cross-checked against attendance and mid-day sign-outs, with route colours matching the sticks children carry, today-only changes that stamp who made them, and a ramp / classroom / walk-up view for each job.

Replaces: a transportation report, a paper attendance list and a call to the office, read against each other at the ramp.
Launch demo →
🚍

Driver Routes

The route sheet a bus driver or substitute opens on their own phone — no school email, no Google account. A link plus a short PIN gets them their route in stop order: who to expect at each stop, who not to wait for, the bus number and stick colour, and a tick list as children board. The transportation office gets the other half: who is actually using it, a full activity log, PIN resets, and the stop editor. Adoption is partial by design, so a route nobody has checked reads “Not checked”, never “0 boarded”.

Replaces: a student-name-sorted list handed to a substitute who has never driven the route, and no way to tell whether a child actually got on.
Launch demo →
🗓️

Parent-Teacher Conferences

One sign-up for the whole family. A parent arrives from the school portal already signed in, sees every child's classes as paper-style sheets, picks times across all of them with a warning if two children collide, and saves once. Who they should meet is worked out from the SIS by grade: the classroom teacher in K–3, the core teachers in 4–6, every class in 7–12. Teachers block their own unavailable times on a grid that refuses to block a slot a parent already holds.

Replaces: a paper sign-up sheet outside each classroom door, and a parent of three juggling thirteen teachers by email.

MACS Fine Arts

Registration for a multi-school fine arts competition the school hosts. Each school signs in with a one-time emailed code, enters its students and groups across 70-plus Bible, music, speech and art categories (with rosters for ensembles and PDF uploads for art), and submits once. The admin console edits every category, field template, room, judge and school the form is built from, shows each submission with its automatic cross-references, and archives the year.

Replaces: a JotForm with free-text "also entered in" boxes and a staff member rebuilding the day grid by hand.
