Memo Check never changes Campus Control. Set up & approve lets you adjust the event (title, times, how early the doors open, which groups) before it records your decision and takes you to Eagle Eye to make the clicks; the row marks itself applied once the plan shows the change (checked hourly). Deny with a reason and the same proposal will not be raised again. An approved row can be re-edited with Edit setup until it is applied.
